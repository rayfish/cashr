# Cashr

A Cashu wallet and Nostr signer for your macOS menu bar.

<img src=".github/images/cashr.png" alt="Cashr wallet showing its balance, Receive, Send, and Nostr actions, and recent payments" width="400">

- Create or restore a wallet and Nostr identity from a 12-word recovery phrase.
- Send and receive Cashu tokens and pay Lightning invoices.
- Connect Nostr apps with bunker links, Nostr Connect, or QR codes.
- Approve signing requests in the app or macOS notifications.
- Manage permissions per app, including **Allow all**, **Deny all**, and **Forget all**.
- Connect apps for zaps with Nostr Wallet Connect.
- Set up a Lightning address or claim a readable name with npub.cash.
- Unlock with Touch ID or your Mac login password.
- Scan QR codes from your screen or clipboard.
- Manage multiple accounts and mints.
